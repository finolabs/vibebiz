- **Licensee**: the business named in the written grant (the "Grant").

## 2. Who needs a commercial license

You need one if you are a company, a sole trader or any other business and you use vibebiz for the business's work, including internal use, use for clients, or as part of a paid service. This applies even if you receive vibebiz for free. Personal study, hobby projects and use by qualifying charitable, educational, public-research, public-safety, environmental or government organizations fall under `LICENSE` and need no commercial license.

## 3. Two kinds of Grant

**Free commercial Grant.** Fioenix may give a business a commercial license at no charge (for example for a pilot). A free Grant is a license, not a waiver: it still must be in writing.

**Paid commercial Grant.** A business pays the fee agreed in the Grant. This document sets no prices; the fee is stated in each Grant.

## 4. What every Grant states

Each Grant must name:

1. **Business**: the legal name of the licensee, and any affiliates covered.
2. **Term**: start date, end date or renewal rule, and how either side may end it early.
3. **Scope**: the number of users or workspaces, the permitted use (internal only, or also for clients), and whether the licensee may modify the plugin for its own use.
4. **Update rights**: which versions the licensee may use. By default, a Grant covers the versions released during its term; whether later major versions are included must be written in the Grant.
5. **Fee**: none (free Grant) or the agreed amount and payment terms (paid Grant).

## 5. What a Grant does not give

- No right to call the licensee's product "vibebiz", or to use vibebiz as Spec Kit branding or anyone else's branding.
- No right to resell, sublicense or redistribute vibebiz to third parties, unless the Grant says so.
- No transfer of copyright. The licensor keeps all rights not expressly granted.
- No warranty beyond the Grant. Unless the Grant says otherwise, vibebiz is provided as is.

## 6. After a Grant ends

When the term ends and no renewal is agreed, the licensee stops using vibebiz for business purposes. Documents the licensee created with vibebiz (its ideas, specs and exports) belong to the licensee and stay usable; the plugin itself may no longer be used commercially.
