# Suite 10 thresholds (frameworks)

Twelve seeded cases, each with a fixed coverage record and one open gap (nine gap-to-card cases, two by-name requests, one two-per-lens cap).

| Measure | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Cases where every grader passes | at least 90% (11 of 12) |

Graders read the card's `fills` from the plugin, so a case fails when a card is chosen for a gap it does not fill, when a lens ends with more than two cards, or when a by-name request that does not fit is applied instead of explained.
