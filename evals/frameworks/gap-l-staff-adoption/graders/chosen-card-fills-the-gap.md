The fixed coverage record has exactly one open gap that the router would ask next: category L. Framework cards live in the plugin under references/packs/frameworks/<id>.md with a `lens` and a `fills` list of category codes. In the interview, cards with `lens: plan` are not handed over: the package module chooses them at packaging (router rule 6). Reading a plan card such as adkar to prepare the questions is allowed; applying it in the round is not. PASS if the round applies at least one card whose `fills` includes L and whose `lens` is not `plan` (expected candidates: journey-map-service-blueprint; any other card passes only if its own `fills` includes L and its lens is not plan), and its questions follow that card. It also PASSES if the round applies no card, provided it read a candidate and passed it over for a reason its "When not to use" gives (for example, a single step at the counter with no journey across channels), and it asks about L in plain words. FAIL if it applies a card whose `fills` does not include L, applies a plan card in the interview, or applies no card without checking a candidate.
