## Framework origins (credits)

The fifteen framework cards in `packs/frameworks/` describe well-known frameworks in vibebiz's own words. No text, layout or artwork is copied. Origins, as commonly cited, are credited here; where a framework has a published source with its own license (for example Strategyzer material under CC BY-SA), vibebiz does not reuse it.

| # | Framework | Origin, as commonly cited |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 Whys | Sakichi Toyoda; developed in the Toyota Production System |
| 2 | Fishbone (Ishikawa diagram) | Kaoru Ishikawa |
| 3 | Jobs to be done | Clayton Christensen, Anthony Ulwick, Bob Moesta and others |
| 4 | Driver tree (KPI tree) | General management-accounting practice; no single originator |
| 5 | Balanced Scorecard | Robert S. Kaplan and David P. Norton |
| 6 | SWOT | Commonly attributed to Albert Humphrey (Stanford Research Institute) |
| 7 | Pre-mortem | Gary Klein |
| 8 | Assumption map | Assumption mapping as described by David J. Bland and Alexander Osterwalder (Strategyzer, *Testing Business Ideas*) |
| 9 | Test-and-control pilot | Controlled experiment design (R. A. Fisher and later retail test-versus-control practice) |
| 10 | Customer journey map and service blueprint | Service blueprint: G. Lynn Shostack; journey mapping: service-design practice |
| 11 | 4P / 7P marketing mix | 4P: E. Jerome McCarthy; 7P extension: Bernard Booms and Mary Jo Bitner |
| 12 | Build-buy-partner with ROI and payback | Corporate-strategy practice; see Laurence Capron and Will Mitchell, *Build, Borrow, or Buy* |
| 13 | MoSCoW | Dai Clegg (DSDM method) |
| 14 | ADKAR | Jeff Hiatt (Prosci) |
| 15 | Cynefin | Dave Snowden |

If you are an originator or rights holder and want a credit corrected, open an issue.
