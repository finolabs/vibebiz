# Test-and-control pilot

## When to use
- The user wants proof the idea works before a full rollout, and the riskiest assumption can be checked in some stores or for some customers.
- The outcome is a number that already has a baseline.
- The board needs a decision point: expand, change or stop.

## When not to use
- No baseline exists and no one can measure it: write a measurement plan first.
- The idea cannot be limited to some stores or customers (a company-wide system change).
- The change is cheap and fully reversible; ship it and watch the number.

## Inputs
- The outcome number, its baseline and the guardrail.
- The list of stores or customer groups that could take part.
- Season calendar and planned promotions.

## Questions
Compare pilot stores (with the idea) against control stores (without it) that are alike in size, location type and sales level.
1. "Bạn có thể thử ở bao nhiêu cửa hàng, và có cửa hàng tương tự để làm nhóm đối chứng không?" Options as ranges, for example "3 cửa hàng thử, 3 cửa hàng đối chứng (Đề xuất)".
2. "Cần chạy tối thiểu bao lâu?" Recommend at least 4 to 8 weeks, long enough to cover a full weekly cycle and not a single sale event.
3. "Trong thời gian thử có đợt khuyến mãi, ngày lễ hay thay đổi mùa nào làm lệch kết quả không?" Plan to pause or compare around it.
4. "Kết quả thế nào thì mở rộng, thay đổi hay dừng?" Fix the three thresholds before the pilot starts.
5. "Ai đọc số liệu hàng tuần và ai quyết định cuối pilot?"

## Writes to
- The plan: pilot stores, control stores, duration, measure, thresholds and decision date.
- Cost and risk: pilot cost as a separate line.
- Outcome: the success and kill thresholds, with a measured or assumed label.
- A diagram brief (line, pilot against control over time).

## Common misuse
- No control group, so a seasonal rise looks like success.
- Choosing the best-performing stores for the pilot.
- Ending the pilot early because early numbers look good.
- Changing the thresholds after seeing the results.
- A pilot shorter than a season check allows: a summer test does not predict the year-end sale.

## Fashion example (fictional)
Chuỗi Lụa Xanh tests a fitting-room queue tool in 4 of 24 stores, with 4 similar stores as control, for 6 weeks outside the Tết sales. Measure: conversion, baseline 18%. Expand if pilot stores beat control by at least 1.5 points; change if between 0.5 and 1.5; stop below 0.5 or if units per transaction fall under 1.9. Then repeat for 2 weeks at year-end to confirm the season effect.
