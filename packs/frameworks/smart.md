# SMART goals

Turns a goal that is already written but vague ("giảm tồn kho nhiều hơn", "bán hết sớm hơn") into one that names exactly what changes, the number and source it is measured by, a target the team can reach, the link to the problem, and a date. Each sharpened goal also gets one owner. It works goal by goal on what the files already hold, and adds no new goals.

## When to use

- C or O holds a goal or key results with no number, no date or no owner.
- The measure is known or easy to name, and only the wording of the goal is loose.
- The board will ask "đạt bao nhiêu, đến khi nào, ai chịu trách nhiệm".

## When not to use

- Several teams share one idea for a period and need one objective with a few key results: use `okr`.
- No goal is written yet and the question is which result the idea moves: build the outcome first with the lens's own steps or `driver-tree`.
- The idea is a compliance fix with a pass or fail result; sharpening adds nothing.

## Inputs

- Each goal or key result in the files, word for word.
- Any baseline already in the files, with source and period.
- The teams named in B, as owners to offer.

## Questions

Take the goals one at a time, at most four, in one round. Offer a sharpened draft of each as an option.

1. "Với mục tiêu “…”, con số nào cho biết nó đã đạt, và lấy từ báo cáo nào?" (Offer a draft measure from the fashion pack, for example "Tỷ lệ hàng tồn cuối mùa trên tổng hàng nhập".)
2. "Con số đó hiện là bao nhiêu, của giai đoạn nào?" (a fact to look up first; without one, record a measurement plan)
3. "Cần đạt bao nhiêu và đến ngày nào? Mức đó có làm được với người và ngân sách hiện có không?" (the target is the user's decision)
4. "Ai chịu trách nhiệm cho con số này?"

## Writes to

- Outcome and measurement (C): each goal rewritten with its measure, baseline or measurement plan, target, date and owner.
- Completion signals (O): each sharpened goal as a Measurable Outcome with its number and source.
- Assumptions: a target the user calls achievable without evidence, labelled as such.
- Diagram brief: none. Show the goals as a table with the columns Goal, Measure and source, Baseline, Target, Date, Owner.

## Common misuse

- Rewriting the goal into a different goal; sharpen what is written and record any change as a decision.
- Treating "achievable" as "easy"; it means reachable with the people and budget named.
- Setting a date with no reason; ask what the date depends on (season, launch, budget cycle).
- Inventing a baseline or target. Record only figures the user or the files give, with source and period.

## Fashion example (fictional)

The fictional chain "Hạ Vàng" wrote "bán hết hàng mùa mà không phải giảm giá sâu" with three loose key results. Sharpened, the first reads: "Tỷ lệ hàng tồn cuối mùa thu đông 2027 trên tổng hàng nhập, từ báo cáo tồn kho, giảm từ mức của mùa 2026 xuống mức nhóm mua hàng chốt, trước 31/01/2028; người chịu trách nhiệm: trưởng nhóm mua hàng". The 2026 baseline is not in the files yet and becomes a measurement plan.
