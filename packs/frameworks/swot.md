# SWOT

## When to use
- The board expects a strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats view of the idea, and the alternatives or risks are still thin.
- The user is optimistic and has listed only advantages.
- You need to place the idea in the company's real situation (stores, suppliers, competitors, season).

## When not to use
- The user needs to find the single riskiest belief: use `assumption-map`.
- The risk is a specific rollout failure: use `pre-mortem`.
- A narrow tactical change where a full four-box view adds nothing.

## Inputs
- The idea in one sentence and the alternatives already recorded.
- Company facts from the ground rules and the fashion pack.

## Questions
Strengths and weaknesses are about the company; opportunities and threats are outside it. Always ask weaknesses and threats before strengths and opportunities.
1. "Điều gì trong công ty khiến ý tưởng này khó làm tốt?" (weakness; open question, a real example)
2. "Bên ngoài công ty có điều gì có thể làm ý tưởng này kém hiệu quả: đối thủ, mùa vụ, nhà cung cấp, quy định?" Options drawn from the fashion pack, multi-select.
3. "Công ty đang có lợi thế gì mà đối thủ khó có?"
4. "Có cơ hội bên ngoài nào chỉ tồn tại trong vài tháng tới, ví dụ mùa Tết hay tựu trường?"
5. Ask which weakness or threat would make the user choose another alternative.

## Writes to
- The alternatives section: why an alternative wins or loses.
- Cost and risk: weaknesses and threats with a likelihood and impact note.
- Decisions, for any weakness or threat the user dismisses, with the reason.
- A diagram brief (quadrant) when the board wants the four boxes.

## Common misuse
- Four long lists with no link to a decision.
- Placing internal items under opportunities or threats.
- Praise-only strengths with no evidence.
- Stopping at the list; each weakness and threat should lead to a question or a recorded decision.

## Fashion example (fictional)
Chuỗi Lụa Xanh considers a size-recommendation tool. Weakness: size data exists for only 40% of products. Threat: a competitor launches a free fit tool before Tết. Strength: 60,000 repeat customers with purchase history. Opportunity: the back-to-school season in August. The weakness changes the alternatives: clean the size data first (fix the process) before buying a tool.
