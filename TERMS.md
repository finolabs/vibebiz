## 1. What vibebiz is

vibebiz is a Claude Cowork plugin that interviews you about an idea and packages it as a requirements document (PRD) for your board and your tech team. It is a thinking and drafting aid. It does not give legal, financial, tax or engineering advice.

## 2. You decide
