# Porter Five Forces

A check of the market around an idea: how hard rivals fight, how much say suppliers and buyers have, what customers could use instead, and how easily new sellers can come in. It tests whether an idea's result depends on players outside the company, and which alternative holds up against them.

## When to use

- The idea's result depends on outside market players: rival shops or brands, a few suppliers who can raise prices, buyers who switch for a small price gap, substitutes, or new sellers entering (a marketplace channel, a private label, a new category).
- Alternatives (D) are open and should be judged by how each one stands against those players; or risks (K) are open and come from the market rather than from the company's own rollout.

## When not to use

- The idea is an internal operations change with no market player involved (timekeeping, stock counts): the board's view of the company's own strengths and weaknesses fits better with `swot`.
- The worry is the wider environment the whole industry shares (laws, taxes, exchange rates, social trends): use `pestel`.
- The market players are already described and the open question is one rollout failure: that belongs to another challenge card, not this one.

## Inputs

- The problem, the outcome and the alternatives already on file.
- The rivals, suppliers, buyer groups and channels the user names; fashion pack facts on seasons and channels.

## Questions

Ask about the players the user can name, one recent example each. Plain words, one round at most; skip a question the files already answer.

1. "Khách đang so sánh mình với những shop hay nhãn nào, và họ hơn mình ở điểm gì: giá, mẫu, giao hàng?"
2. "Nguồn hàng hoặc nguyên liệu chính đang phụ thuộc vào bao nhiêu nhà cung cấp? Gần đây họ có tăng giá hay đổi điều kiện không?"
3. "Khách có dễ bỏ sang chỗ khác không? Lần gần nhất mất khách vì lý do gì?"
4. "Ngoài sản phẩm giống mình, khách còn cách nào khác để có được thứ họ cần (mua đồ cũ, thuê, tự may)?"
5. "Có nhãn hay người bán mới nào vừa vào thị trường này, hoặc sắp vào?"
6. Ask which of these pressures would make the user prefer another alternative, offering the alternatives on file as options.

## Writes to

- Alternatives (D): for each alternative, which outside pressure it eases or worsens, and why one wins.
- Cost & Risk (K): market threats with the player behind each, a likelihood and impact note, and the user's source.
- Decisions: any pressure the user dismisses, with the reason.
- Diagram brief (radar): the five pressures rated low, medium or high from the user's answers; a pressure without an answer shows as "not yet clarified".

## Common misuse

- Rating a pressure "high" from general knowledge; rate only from what the user says, and record a missing view as an open question.
- Listing the five pressures with no link to a choice; each one should strengthen or weaken an alternative.
- Inventing market shares, price gaps or supplier counts. Record only figures the user gives, with their source.

## Fashion example (fictional)

The fictional chain "Gió Nam" plans a cheap private-label T-shirt for a marketplace. Rivals sell similar shirts about 30% cheaper; only two mills supply the cotton and both raised prices; buyers switch shops for a small gap. The check shows the private label faces strong pressure from rivals, suppliers and buyers at once, so the alternative "a basic shirt with a size guarantee the rivals do not offer" is recorded beside the cheap shirt for the board to compare.
