# Cost of inaction

Doing nothing is an alternative with a price. This card makes that price visible: what keeping things as they are costs each month or season, what waiting a year adds, and which part of it nobody has measured yet. It answers the board's "why not leave it as it is?" with the user's own figures, never with invented ones.

## When to use

- The board or the user treats "do nothing" as free, or asks why now and why not wait.
- Alternatives (D) are open and "do nothing" has no cost written next to it; or Cost, resources and return (M) is open and the return of the idea rests on what the status quo loses.
- The user can name where the status quo leaks: markdowns, stock-outs, staff hours, lost customers, penalties, rework.

## When not to use

- The cost of doing nothing is already on file and the board has agreed to act; the open question is which route to take and what each costs: `build-buy-partner-roi` fits better, and this card's result feeds it.
- Nobody can name a single place where the status quo loses money or time: frame the problem first (A is not clear).

## Inputs

- The problem statement and its figures with sources, the outcome and its baseline.
- The ground rules' priorities and approval ceiling.

## Questions

Ask for leaks the user has seen, one recent period each. Plain words, one round at most. A figure the user does not have becomes a measurement plan or an open question with its owner; never fill it in.

1. "Nếu để nguyên như bây giờ, công ty đang mất ở đâu: hàng phải giảm giá, hết hàng mất đơn, giờ công làm tay, khách bỏ đi, hay chỗ khác?" (Multi-select from the leaks the files name.)
2. "Với mỗi chỗ đó, kỳ gần nhất mất khoảng bao nhiêu, và số đó lấy từ đâu?" (Offer "chưa có số, cần đo" as an option.)
3. "Nếu chờ thêm một năm mới làm, chỗ nào sẽ nặng hơn: thêm cửa hàng, thêm mùa, đối thủ đi trước?"
4. "Có cái giá nào không đo bằng tiền mà ban điều hành vẫn quan tâm: uy tín, nhân viên nghỉ việc, rủi ro sai sót?"

## Writes to

- Alternatives (D): "do nothing" with its cost per period as a low, base and high range built only from the user's figures, each with source and period, labelled "not tech-reviewed".
- Cost & Risk (M): the cost of waiting one more year, as the same range, and the unmeasured leaks as measurement plans.
- Open questions: each figure nobody present can give, with the person who can.
- Diagram brief (bar): one bar per leak for the status quo; a leak without a figure shows as "not yet clarified", never as zero.

## Common misuse

- Adding an industry average or a guessed figure to make the case stronger. Only the user's figures with a source go in; the rest stays an open question.
- Presenting one precise total; give a range and keep every input visible.
- Counting the same loss twice (lost sales and the margin on those sales).
- Using the card to argue for the idea; it prices the status quo, and the board still compares it with the other routes.

## Fashion example (fictional)

The fictional chain "Lụa Xanh" orders each season by feel. The buying team's end-of-season report shows about 18% of shirts marked down by half (measured, summer 2026). The user also names stock-outs of best sellers mid-season but has no figure. The card records the markdown cost range from the report, turns the stock-out loss into a measurement plan owned by the stores, and notes that waiting a year adds two more seasons of both. The board sees "do nothing" with a price and one open figure.
