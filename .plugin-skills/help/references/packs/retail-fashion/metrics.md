# Fashion retail metrics

Plain definition, formula, how to read it. Always confirm the user's own definition before recording a number, and record the unit, the period and the source with every figure.

| Metric | Plain definition | Formula | Reading notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sell-through rate | Share of the units bought that have been sold in a period | units sold ÷ (units sold + units on hand at the end) | Fix the period (for example the first 8 weeks, or the whole season). Compare styles of the same age only. |
| GMROI (gross margin return on inventory investment) | Gross margin earned for each đồng of stock held, valued at cost | gross margin ÷ average stock value at cost | Above 1 means stock earns more than it costs. Use the same period for both parts. |
| Open-to-buy | Buying budget still free for a period | planned sales + planned end stock − stock on hand − orders already placed (same price basis) | Negative means over-committed. Needs a sales plan the user trusts. |
| Markdown rate | Share of sales value given up in price cuts | (original price value − actual selling value) ÷ original price value, on units sold | Say whether promotions and vouchers are included. |
| Size-curve fit | How well the sizes bought match the sizes sold | for each size, compare its share of units sold with its share of units bought; sum the gaps | Read per style group. A style sold out in size M and full in XL has a poor fit even when overall sell-through looks fine. |
| Conversion rate | Share of visitors who buy | orders ÷ visitors (website sessions, or shop footfall) | State the denominator: sessions, unique visitors or footfall. Shops and web are not comparable without that. |
| Units per transaction | Items per receipt | units sold ÷ number of transactions | Moves up with bundles and "buy 2" offers. |
| Average selling price | Average price actually paid per unit | net sales value ÷ units sold | Falls when discounting rises or the mix shifts to cheaper items; separate the two causes. |
| Footfall | People entering a shop | door counter total for the period | Needs a counter or a manual count; staff may be counted by mistake. |
| RFM segments | Customers grouped by how recently they bought, how often, and how much | rank each customer on recency, frequency and money spent; group the ranks (for example loyal, new, at risk, lost) | Needs customers identified at checkout. A shop that cannot link receipts to people cannot compute it. |
| Customer lifetime value | Gross margin a customer brings over the whole relationship | average margin per order × orders per year × expected years active | An estimate; state the assumed years. Use margin, not revenue. |
| Return rate | Share of sold units or orders sent back | units returned ÷ units sold (or orders returned ÷ orders), same period and channel | Split by reason (size, fit, defect, changed mind) and by channel. |

## Related checks

- Gross margin after markdowns, returns and delivery is the number a board cares about; revenue alone hides losses.
- When two metrics share a part (for example sell-through and markdown rate), say so, so that a gain in one is not claimed twice.
