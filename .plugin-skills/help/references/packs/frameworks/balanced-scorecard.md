# Balanced Scorecard

## When to use
- The idea must be tied to the company's strategy, and the ground rules hold a strategy map or yearly objectives.
- The outcome looks purely financial and the user should see the customer, process and people effects.
- The board reads results through the four perspectives: financial, customer, internal process, learning and growth.

## When not to use
- The ground rules hold no objectives and the user cannot name the company's priorities; ask for the one priority this idea serves instead.
- A small, single-store idea where four perspectives would be ceremony.
- The outcome is already clear and measured; use `driver-tree` for the numbers.

## Inputs
- The organization's objectives from the ground rules, if present.
- The outcome already recorded for the idea.

## Questions
Check which perspective the idea moves first, then the others as side effects.
1. "Ý tưởng này phục vụ mục tiêu nào của công ty năm nay?" If objectives are on file, offer them as options and recommend the closest one; otherwise ask as an open question.
2. "Nếu thành công, điều thay đổi rõ nhất là gì?" Options: "Doanh thu hoặc chi phí (Đề xuất)", "Khách hài lòng hoặc quay lại nhiều hơn", "Quy trình trong cửa hàng nhanh hơn", "Nhân viên làm việc tốt hơn".
3. "Một thay đổi nào đó có thể xấu đi ở góc nhìn còn lại không?" For example, faster checkout but staff lose selling time.
4. "Ai trong công ty theo dõi con số này hàng tháng?" This gives the owner of the measure.

## Writes to
- The outcome section: the main perspective, secondary effects and one measure each.
- The risk part of the spec when a perspective is harmed.
- Decisions, when the user links the idea to a named company objective.

## Common misuse
- Filling all four perspectives with vague claims; two honest ones are better than four invented ones.
- Treating the scorecard as a list of measures without a link to the strategy.
- Ignoring the learning and growth perspective, which is where staff adoption sits.
- Copying objectives the user never stated.

## Fashion example (fictional)
Chuỗi Lụa Xanh has a yearly objective "tăng khách quay lại mua lần hai". A win-back reminder idea moves the customer perspective first (repeat purchase rate), then financial (revenue from existing customers). Internal process: one person spends 2 hours a day sending messages by hand today. Learning and growth: store staff need 1 hour of training. The harm to watch: unsubscribe rate on the customer side.
