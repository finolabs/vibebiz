# PESTEL

A scan of the outside environment the company cannot control: politics and trade, the economy, society and buying habits, technology, environment, and law. It turns a vague worry about "things outside our hands" into named risks, each with an owner who watches it.

## When to use

- Risk and compliance (K) is open, and the worry comes from outside the company: laws in one or more countries, taxes and duties, exchange rates, changing buying habits, platform or payment technology, environmental or labelling rules, trade relations.
- The idea crosses a border, a new channel or a regulated area (personal data, promotions, imported goods) where outside rules decide whether it can run.

## When not to use

- The pressure comes from named market players (rivals, suppliers, buyers, new sellers): use `five-forces`.
- The board wants the company's own strengths and weaknesses beside outside opportunities and threats: use `swot`, which this card can feed.
- The worry is a concrete rollout failure inside the company (staff not trained, store network down): `pre-mortem` fits better.

## Inputs

- The idea, its scope (E) and its data (G).
- The countries, channels and rules the user names; the ground rules' mandatory approvals; the fashion pack's promotion rules.

## Questions

Ask only the factors the idea touches; skip the rest. Plain words, one round at most.

1. "Việc này phải tuân theo luật hay quy định nào mà anh/chị biết: dữ liệu khách hàng, khuyến mãi, nhãn mác, hàng nhập khẩu? Ai trong công ty đang theo dõi chuyện đó?"
2. "Thuế, phí hay tỷ giá thay đổi thì việc này bị ảnh hưởng thế nào? Đã từng gặp lần nào chưa?"
3. "Thói quen mua sắm của nhóm khách này có gì khác hoặc đang thay đổi không: kênh mua, cách trả tiền, mùa mua sắm?"
4. "Có thay đổi nào về công nghệ hay nền tảng bên ngoài (sàn, cổng thanh toán, đơn vị vận chuyển) có thể làm việc này dừng lại không?"
5. "Có yêu cầu nào về môi trường hay bao bì, hoặc quan hệ thương mại giữa các nước, có thể ảnh hưởng không?"

## Writes to

- Cost & Risk (K): one risk per factor the user confirms, with its source, likelihood and impact note, and who watches it.
- Open questions: each rule nobody present can confirm, with the person or body who can.
- Assumptions: any outside condition the plan relies on (a tax rate, a rule staying unchanged), marked unconfirmed.
- Diagram brief: none. Show a table with the columns Factor, What could change, Effect on the idea, Who watches it.

## Common misuse

- Filling all six factors with generic points; keep only factors the idea actually touches.
- Stating a law, tax rate or exchange rate from memory. Record only what the user states or a document on file says, with its source; the rest becomes an open question.
- Treating the scan as legal advice; it lists what to check, and the check belongs to the person named.

## Fashion example (fictional)

The fictional chain "Mây Hồng" wants to sell online to customers in a neighbouring country. The scan names four risks: personal-data rules in both countries (owner: the legal officer, open question), import duty on clothing (owner: accounting, rate not yet confirmed), the exchange rate moving prices weekly (assumption: prices set in local currency once a month), and buyers there preferring cash on delivery (open question for the courier). Each lands in Cost & Risk with its owner.
