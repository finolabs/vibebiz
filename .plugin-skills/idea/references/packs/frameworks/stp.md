# Segmentation, targeting and positioning (STP)

Three moves in order. Split the buyers into a few groups that buy for different reasons; pick the one group the idea serves first; then say in one sentence what the offer means to that group compared with what it buys today. The chosen group decides what the first release holds.

## When to use

- The idea is a new range, offer or service and its buyers (B) are still "everyone" or several groups with no first choice.
- Teams disagree about who the idea is for, and the scope (E) depends on that choice: which items, sizes, price band or channel go first.
- The board will ask "who exactly buys this and why from us", and the files hold no answer.

## When not to use

- The target group and position are already chosen and recorded; the open work is price, channel, promotion or service: `marketing-mix-4p-7p` fits better.
- The idea is internal (stock, reporting, store operations) and has no buyer to choose.
- The problem is unclear (A is not Clear): choosing a group around an unproven problem fixes the wrong thing.

## Inputs

- The groups of buyers the user or the teams name, in their own words.
- Any sales or customer report on file that splits buyers by age, channel, region, occasion or spend, with its source and period.
- The outcome and its guardrail, from the outcome lens.
- What buyers use today instead: the chain's own range, a competitor's, nothing.

## Questions

Ask about real buyers the user has seen, one round at most. Offer the groups already named in the files as options.

1. "Những nhóm khách nào hiện mua loại hàng này của mình? Kể hai đến bốn nhóm, mỗi nhóm mua vì lý do khác nhau." (Offer the groups the files name.)
2. "Nếu đợt đầu chỉ phục vụ được một nhóm, nhóm nào nên đi trước, và vì sao?" (Recommend the group with a sales figure on file; never rank groups by a figure nobody gave.)
3. "Hiện nhóm đó đang mua thứ này ở đâu, hoặc dùng gì thay thế?"
4. "Với nhóm đó, mình muốn họ nhớ món hàng này bằng một câu nào: khác gì so với thứ họ đang mua?"
5. "Chọn nhóm đó thì đợt đầu cần có những gì, và bỏ lại những gì cho sau?"

A group size, share or spend nobody present can give becomes a measurement plan or an open question (ask protocol rules 5 and 10), never an estimate of yours.

## Writes to

- Users and stakeholders (B): the buyer groups, the chosen target group and why, the groups left for later.
- Scope and behavior (E): what the first release holds for the target group (items, sizes, price band, channel) and what waits.
- Decisions: the chosen group and the one-sentence position, as an `answer` entry when the user gave them.
- Diagram brief: none. Show a table with the columns Group, Why they buy, Buys today from, Chosen (yes or later).

## Common misuse

- Splitting by age or region alone when the groups buy for the same reason; a segment differs in why or how it buys.
- Choosing every group "to be safe"; the point is one first group.
- Writing a slogan instead of a position; the position compares the offer with what the group buys today.
- Inventing segment sizes or spend to rank groups. Use only figures on file with their source.

## Fashion example (fictional)

The fictional chain "Gió Bấc" wants a mid-priced winter jacket range. The teams name three groups:

| Group | Why they buy | Buys today from | Chosen |
|---|---|---|---|
| Office workers 25–35 | A neat jacket for the commute | Imported brands, on sale | Yes |
| New graduates | A first jacket on a small budget | Online marketplaces | Later |
| Parents buying for the family | Warmth for several people at once | Supermarkets | Later |

Position: "a neat, warm work jacket at a price below the imported brands". The first drop then holds three cuts in office colours, sold in the city stores and online; family packs wait for a later season.
